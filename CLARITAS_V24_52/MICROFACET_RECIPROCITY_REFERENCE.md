# Microfacet Reciprocity Reference — V24.52

V24.52 retains the V24.42 reciprocity validation unchanged in physical meaning. The complete production-boundary test still covers 28 kaolin and 38 loess Qsca>=1 bins (66 total), with a bin failing only when the relative discrepancy is greater than 1% **and** at least 5 sigma. Cross-interface ordinary, near-critical and TIR/grazing states remain compulsory.

The boundary implementation follows the Smith/Beckmann visible-normal and multiple-scattering framework described by Heitz & d'Eon (2014, DOI 10.1111/cgf.12417) and Heitz et al. (2016, DOI 10.1145/2897824.2925943). V24.52 does not alter the intended VNDF or multiple-scattering distribution.

V24.52 adds an independent second layer: even a complete boundary reciprocity PASS cannot authorize sediment transport until the exact full-production million-ray qualification has zero failures.
