CORE V24.49 CHANGE — LOCAL REPLAY VALIDATION
======================================================================

Replace exact global float32-vs-float64 long-orbit equality as a release-blocking geometry criterion with a LOCAL INDEPENDENT REPLAY CHECK.

The principle is:

For each residual CUDA trajectory, inspect the state actually held by CUDA immediately before the first meaningful point of disagreement.

From THAT CUDA state, independently recompute on the host/reference side:

- current physical region;
- candidate boundary intersections;
- validity of finite surfaces;
- nearest valid physical surface;
- intersection distance;
- intersection coordinates;
- boundary classification;
- side of interface;
- material transition;
- surface normal;
- incidence geometry;
- refractive-index pair;
- critical-angle condition;
- TIR/non-TIR decision;
- internal acrylic/acrylic continuation;
- expected RNG requirement or non-requirement;
- applicable numerical tolerances.

Then compare this local reference result against the actual CUDA decision.

The host reference must be independently implemented.

Do NOT simply call CUDA production helpers from Python.

======================================================================
LOCAL REPLAY ACCEPTANCE CRITERION
======================================================================

For a residual case to pass:

1. The actual CUDA pre-interaction state must be finite and valid.

2. The independent high-precision local reference must agree that CUDA selected a physically valid next boundary from that state.

3. The selected surface/interface classification must be physically correct.

4. Any TIR / transmission / reflection classification relevant to the divergence must be correct within explicitly documented numerical tolerance.

5. No impossible region transition may occur.

6. No internal acrylic/acrylic continuation may be misclassified as an optical interface.

7. No unjustified RNG draw may occur at internal acrylic/acrylic continuation.

8. No NaN/Inf or invalid numerical state may be present.

Interaction count from a separately propagated double-precision global trajectory is NOT itself a pass/fail condition.

A global orbit may diverge numerically while every individual CUDA decision remains locally correct.
